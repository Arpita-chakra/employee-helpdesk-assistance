
EMPLOYEE LEAVE POLICY

1. Annual Leave
All eligible employees receive 18 days of annual paid leave
per calendar year. Employees should request annual leave
in advance and obtain approval from their manager.

2. Leave Carry-Forward
Employees may carry forward a maximum of 5 unused
annual leave days into the next calendar year.
Any additional carry-forward requires a separate
written exception from HR.

3. Sick Leave
Eligible employees receive 10 days of sick leave per year.
Employees should inform their manager when they are
unable to work due to illness.

4. Public Holidays
Official company holidays are announced by HR.
Public holidays are separate from annual leave and
are not deducted from the annual leave balance.

5. Leave Approval
Leave requests are subject to business requirements.
Submitting a leave request does not automatically
mean that it has been approved.
